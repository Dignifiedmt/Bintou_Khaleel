About Me

The Heart Behind the Hustle

If you want to know who I am at my core, look at the beautiful intersection of health education, digital innovation, business and community empowerment.

I am Fatima Ibrahim, popularly known and addressed as Bintou Khaleel, an educator, health care educator, social media manager, digital creator, caterer and kitchen utensil entrepreneur who believes that true fulfilment comes from building a life of purpose, health, independence and resilience.

Many people simply call me Aunty Bintou. I love running home lessons for kids, especially during the holidays, keeping young minds sharp, creative and engaged with fun and productive learning. Beyond the titles, I am passionate about lifelong learning, mentoring children, promoting health awareness, leveraging technology and creating supportive spaces where people can discover their true potential.

I cherish my family, value strong roots, and believe deeply in the power of women supporting women.

What I Do and What I Know

Four worlds, one purpose

My journey is built on a diverse foundation of knowledge and practical experience, where every role feeds the next.

Health and Academic Education

As a health care educator and classroom or online tutor, I am deeply passionate about public health, environmental wellness, and sharing vital knowledge that keeps communities informed, healthy and thriving. I also run holiday home lessons that keep young minds sharp, creative and engaged.

The Culinary and Kitchen World

Food is care and community, which is why I love my work as a caterer. Alongside that, I run a business supplying quality kitchen utensils, equipping homes with the essential tools they need to bring people together around a table.

The Digital Space and Tech

As a creator and social media manager, I craft digital content and technical presentations. I also actively study artificial intelligence, online safety and the future of work to stay ahead in a rapidly changing world.

Vocational and Business Hub

I founded and manage an initiative where we teach practical vocational and business skills, turning everyday knowledge into sustainable income and opening doors to self reliance.
